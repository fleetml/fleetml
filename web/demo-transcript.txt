
▶ Starting the dev stack and a demo server

▶ 1. Publish three versions of a model
  $ fleetml push models/iris/iris_v1.tflite --model iris --version 1.0.0
  ✓ Pushed models/iris/iris_v1.tflite → iris v1.0.0 (INT8)
  $ fleetml push models/iris/iris_v2.tflite --model iris --version 2.0.0
  ✓ Pushed models/iris/iris_v2.tflite → iris v2.0.0 (INT8)
  $ fleetml push models/iris/iris_v2.tflite --model iris --version 2.1.0
  ✓ Pushed models/iris/iris_v2.tflite → iris v2.1.0 (INT8)
  each file was sha256-verified on upload; only verified files are ever sent to devices

▶ 2. Twenty devices enroll and get 1.0.0
  $ fleetml simulate --devices 20 --label app=demo   # simulated phones: real protocol, real downloads
  $ POST /api/v1/deployments  {version: 1.0.0, target: app=demo}
  fleet: iris 1.0.0 on 20

▶ 3. Drift: the last day's latency against the week before
  latency drift for 1.0.0: NONE (PSI 0.026)
  now the devices slow down (say, after an OS update)...
  latency drift for 1.0.0: CRITICAL (PSI 8.511)
  a change into CRITICAL is sent as an alert: 1 drift alert(s) on NATS

▶ 4. A canary of 2.0.0 with a health gate; 2.0.0 is broken
  $ POST /api/v1/deployments  {version: 2.0.0, strategy: CANARY 25% -> 100%, gate: errors <= 5% over 30 events, else ROLLBACK}
  fleet: iris 1.0.0 on 12, iris 2.0.0 on 8   (the canary cohort only)
  deployment ROLLED_BACK: error rate 22.92% exceeds threshold 5.00% (11/48 events from 8 cohort devices)
  fleet: iris 1.0.0 on 20, nobody outside the canary ever saw 2.0.0

▶ 5. A/B test: 1.0.0 against the fixed 2.1.0
  $ POST /api/v1/experiments  {control: 1.0.0, treatment: 2.1.0, target: app=demo, min_sample_size: 300}
  control 1.0.0:   350 inferences, mean 34 ms
  treatment 2.1.0: 350 inferences, mean 13 ms
  Welch t-test p < 0.0001, recommendation: ADOPT_TREATMENT
  $ POST /api/v1/deployments  {version: 2.1.0, target: app=demo}
  fleet: iris 2.1.0 on 20

▶ Done in 30s (plus 17s of setup)
  The same steps work against your devices with the Android and iOS SDKs (docs/ANDROID_SDK.md, docs/IOS_SDK.md).
